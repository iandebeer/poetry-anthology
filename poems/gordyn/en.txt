From Behind the Curtain
Here – behind the curtain,
through bars and glass,
I see the garden,
with butterflies fluttering over beds of freshly cut grass.
Here – behind the curtain,
the ego hides away with me,
as if to vanish into the void,
yet it stands firm, ready to fight its false existence.
Here – behind the curtain,
alongside me on the windowsill,
lie dead flies and a bumblebee,
sun-dried and silent like echoes once ringing in my head.
Here – behind the curtain,
through bars and glass,
the blue sky calls in bright sunshine:
come out, come play among butterflies and flowers on freshly cut grass.
One, two, three,
here I come, ready or not,
the hiding game's over and done—
block the ego, the garden calls me.

# Do you have a shotgun I can borrow?

Sorry, my wife is a pacifist and she will donner me if I keep a shotgun, but I believe dynamite works just fine – it might seem to be a bit detached from the actual kill to blast the quacking bastards with TNT, but you can always go and wring the necks of the stunned and injured ones afterwards.
Don’t thank me, just bring me a bag of bloody feathers so I can stuff a pillow with it – it’s great for smothering kittens with.

Parody Lost

Ironic juxtaposition is an art,
it seems, I have not mastered
For calling an Egyptian Geese
a quacking bastard

I must apologise, most sincere
to those whose anguish were severe
Of down I will not a cushion make
in feline slaughter to partake

For the silent ones, who face the fire
I express my heart’s earnest desire
That they, who are looking for a gun
Would shoot clay pigeons for their fun

The MU of the Gorge

Swartboskloof stands bare, ablaze —
from the charred heights, above the Helderberg vale
to where soot and dying creatures swirl in the Eerste River’s pool,
as if reluctantly bidding farewell to what once was — what they were;
while smoke from smoldering roots tries to draw a veil
over that now laid bare in blackened nudity,
with only the rocks still offering signposts
in the fractal-mapped emptiness,
imagine-skirting further gorges
Cork-screw and Assegai Bush.

Swartboskloof is clothed in floral splendor —
Watsonia and Arum lilies
stubbornly rise from the still-scorched earth,
coloring the gorge with the promise of blooms to come,
quietly affirmed by a green shoot
from the obviously burned Waboom.
Ferns line the silver streams that gurgle over moss-covered stones,
Your heart leaps with joy at this seemingly instinctive new life.
Yet the gorge knows nothing of more than the eternal now
where it is both emptiness and fullness.

Swartboskloof, with gentle green slopes that wrap around wounds and walls,
with all that is gorge,
undivided and continuous with every ravine
in the Divine awareness of
never emptied, never fulfilled,
forever perfect
and calls:
I AM the Alpha and the Omega!

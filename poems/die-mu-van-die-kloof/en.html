The Mother of the Ravine

Swartboskloof lies scorched and bare —
from the false summit, above the Helderberg vale
down to where soot and lifeless creatures in the Eersteriver whirl
as if they slowly bid farewell to what was — to what they were;
while the smoke of smoldering roots struggles to veil
what now lies revealed in stark black nakedness
with only the rocks still offering beacons in
the fractal-mapped emptiness,
imagined outlined by further gorges
Cork-tree and Assegaaibosch.

Swartboskloof is adorned in floral splendor —
Watsonia and Aaron's lilies
stubbornly sprout from the still scorched earth,
coloring the ravine with the promise of bloom ahead,
quietly affirmed by a green shoot
from the evidently charred Waboom.
Ferns outline the silver streams that rush over mossy stones,
Your heart leaps with joy at what intuitively seems new life,
Yet the ravine knows nothing of more than the eternal moment
in which it is both emptiness and fullness.

Swartboskloof, with soft green undulations that embrace its wounds and walls
with all that is ravine —
undivided and continuous with all gorges
in the Divine awareness of
never emptied, never fulfilled,
always perfect
and calls:
I AM the Alpha and the Omega!

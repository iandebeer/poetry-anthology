# Who Am I?  
  
When you call to me, I am gone.  
If you seek to capture me, there’s nothing to hold.  
Between words, I speak to you.  
Between the notes of a song, my voice rings clear.  
In the rustling of leaves, hear my whisper.  
In the thunder’s mighty roar, I repose.  
Wait on me, and you’ll surely find,  
for those who know me, find peace in mind.

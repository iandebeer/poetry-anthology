Sonnet to the Illusory Timeless (Otter Trail)

Where stunted trees hang over a carpet of leaves
and gnarled roots anchor the path above the sea,
rich cormorants prepare for their morning feast—
as we, as mere bystanders, step into eternity.

In a valley where streams flow with sweet water’s grace
to a deep, flowing river of dark allure,
that dares to push against the ocean's embrace
and with wind and sand, smooths the steadfast shore.

Here is where my footsteps find their laborious way,
and breath feels like fire igniting my chest,
where I can hush my thoughts into tranquil sway
and gratefully reach for a hand that’s the best.

Now back home, I lie clean and cradled in bed;
still, I cling to each step with a quiet dread.

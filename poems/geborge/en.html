Sheltered

Rachmaninoff - a Rhapsody on a Theme of Paganini -
the prelude to "The du Plooys of Soetmelksvlei."
Memories flood my mind
of a time so long past yet so easily recalled;

Our family huddled by the radio,
after the enforced silence of “The News at Seven”
and the respectful hush for “Let’s Take Our Books.”
We listen together to the ups and downs of Uncle Kobie, Aunt Hybie, and old Bartel,
each in the quiet of their own imagined world.
Then off to bed where I lie, listening to Dad’s “Tromp of Diggelin” exercises—silently,
until the quarter to ten alarm tells us;
“The undesirables are off the streets—everything is now safe.”

… strange, for I never felt unsafe in that home.

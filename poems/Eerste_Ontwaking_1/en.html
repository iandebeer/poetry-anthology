First Awakening

It was in drowsy sleep
where a deep yet shining dream,
would lift me up and sweep, as if I'd leap
in an unseen air stream –

I could soar to where the whitest clouds roll
against the blue, blue sky;
and in that moment, let time grow whole
in my unchained flight –

and who could ever know
that in this quiet hour's grace
I am immortal,
in this place.

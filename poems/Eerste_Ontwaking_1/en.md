# First Awakening

On a lazy afternoon I once slept  
and fell into a lucid dream  
in which I freely soared and swept  
on the wind-current's stream –  
above the brightest clouds I soared  
against an azure blue sky;  
untied from time's gripping cord  
in my untethered skyward high –  
an eternal moment of bliss  
in which I could clearly see  
in the ever here of suchness:  
that I-self also immortal be.  
  
So'hum, So'hum, Shivo'hum

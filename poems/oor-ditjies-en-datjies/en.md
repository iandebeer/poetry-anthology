# On This and That

You talk so smoothly of this and that:
you say that that is this which this is not;
and that is this which that knew not.
But when I view the lot from my perspective,
your this begins to look more and more like that to me.
Can we just say, to keep the peace,
the difference between us lies in here and there.

Neither thís, nor thát —
neti neti.

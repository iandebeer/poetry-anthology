In Absentia (Stellenbosch's Dreamers)

to you who are far away,
now that I am no longer there,
please do not ask
to disturb me in the beyond,

for if you seek me, you will surely find —
in Jonkershoek, past the warming fire,
where Swartboskloof's stream will quench our thirst,
on the mountain path where the afternoon sun will shine on our leaves,
in Eden's hollow where moss and ferns embrace the walls,
in the cool shade of the waterfall's spray,
at the foot of Botmanskop, as the sinking sun hangs over Table Mountain,
by Idas Valley's ponds, when the bush shrike calls us with its song —

here I have carried you in my heart,
when the weight was too much
or when joy alone demanded to be shared.

but you will have to venture forth to keep up,
for I now run barefoot,
for I am now free.

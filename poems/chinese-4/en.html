A little cloud shifts from gray to gold,
Igniting the mountain’s spine with flame,
The slender crescent moon glimmers cold,
While the earth spins, almost as if planned, just the same.

To know or to marvel? Often our knowing,
Rooted in science or faith, blocks the sight
Of wonder—such a wall, so unyielding;
Interesting how ‘wonder’ reveals both plight and light.

I marvel at the beauty of this new dawn,
Aware that sunlight dances on clouds, mountains, and moon,
A tapestry woven through the polarization of water,
By photons cast from our local star, the Sun.

Where hydrogen fuses into helium's song,
Releasing residual energy in luminous grace;
It takes eons for light to reach the surface, long,
And then 8 minutes and 19 seconds for its embrace.

The Earth, bound in the Sun's mass, warps space-time,
Orbiting in an ellipse as day turns to night,
Spinning on its axis, a rhythm sublime,
Creating the illusion of a sunrise so bright.

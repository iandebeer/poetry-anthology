Thula Baba — background video (Remotion / anthology)

Place your finished file here as:
  video.mp4   (or video.mov / video.webm — see engine/loadPoems auto-detection)

The player uses ONE background clip per poem. If you have several shots, edit them into one
timeline in your editor (e.g. walk first, then hold or cut to the wide view at the top).

Suggested content (False Bay / Cape Peninsula mood):

1) Path / approach
   - Person walking on a footpath over a dune, fynbos in frame (texture, movement, wind).

2) Reveal (top of dune or cut to summit beat)
   - False Bay opens ahead; sea expanse dominant.
   - Cape Town and Table Mountain on the horizon — readable but distant (atmospheric haze is fine).

Technical hints for a cinematic read:
   - Stable or slow gimbal; avoid harsh midday if you want softer light on the bay.
   - 1920×1080 or higher, h.264 or similar; keep duration in line with poem length + margins in Remotion.

After adding the file, run: npm run generate-poems
Then preview in Remotion Studio or: npm run render -- thula-baba af
